# Data sources

Every number in the app comes from a public archive of NASA, NOAA or USGS, or is calculated from one
with a formula written in [`METHODOLOGY.md`](METHODOLOGY.md). No value is generated by AI.

Where this information lives in the repository:

| What | Where |
|---|---|
| Every downloaded file, with its URL, size and date | `data/raw/mars/MANIFEST.csv`, `data/raw/moon/MANIFEST.csv` |
| Download code (products and URLs) | `src/analogs/download.py`, `src/analogs/mcs.py`, `scripts/download_moon.py`, `scripts/download_earth.py` |
| Source of each variable on each body | `configs/variables.yaml` (`sources`) |
| Source shown on each card of the app | `app/data/<body>/index.json` (`source`, `short_source`) |
| Licences and credits that must be shown | [`../CREDITS.md`](../CREDITS.md) |

> **Citations.** The products are listed as their archives name them. The reference given for each is
> the publication or dataset citation its archive recommends.

## 8. Known data-quality caveats

- **MOLA and LOLA are interpolated between ground tracks**: slopes there are smoother than reality. The
  card shows the fraction of each cell measured by the laser.
- **Mars GRS is ~300 km** and **MERRA-2 dust ~55 km**: regional context, not a way to tell neighbouring
  cells apart.
- **Mars mean temperature** is blocky at 5° (few MCS night samples per bin).
- **Mars temperatures come from two different Mars years** (day: MY26, night: MY31) and two instruments.
- **IMERG overestimates rain in hyper-arid deserts** (Atacama: 80–260 mm/year against a few mm measured).
- **ECOSTRESS silica** is reliable only on bare rock and soil; in vegetated basaltic areas it reads too
  high.
- **Lunar Prospector units** (fraction or %) are not stated clearly in the product label.
- **Diviner PRP code −1** is not documented in the label; it is read as "ice stable at the surface"
  from its temperatures, to be checked against Paige et al. (2010b).
